**Headline: Is your architecture a resource devourer or an efficiency engine? 🌍**

I've seen architectures that need 2GB of RAM just to "exist" before processing a single byte of data. In a world crying out for **Green-IT** and efficiency, that's no longer acceptable.

The "Architect's Awakening" happens when you realize robustness isn't measured in gigabytes consumed, but in your system's ability to stay resilient while consuming as little as possible.

Meet **wpipe**. 🐍

While other orchestration tools (Low-Code or heavy) demand giant containers, **wpipe** orchestrates complex pipelines with just a few **Kilobytes** of RAM.

### 📊 The Impact of wpipe:
*   **Extreme Savings:** We've gone from 2GB environments to ultra-light runs.
*   **Real Traction:** +117,000 downloads prove the community seeks lightness.
*   **Native Resilience:** SQLite Checkpoints let you resume failed processes without losing state.
